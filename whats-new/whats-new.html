Argus Inspector What's new

Faster to start, easier to switch

Three changes aimed at getting you inspecting sooner, with fewer clicks along the way.

Click the icon, skip the popup

Clicking the Argus icon now opens the toolbar directly on the page — no popup in between. The keyboard shortcut is still shown right on the icon when you hover it.

Inspect starts automatically

The toolbar used to open with nothing active. Now Inspect turns on the moment the toolbar appears, so you're hovering elements right away.

Switch tools without the mouse

Hold Alt to see a number over every tool, then press a digit to jump straight to it. Or keep holding Alt and tap ← / → to step through them in order.
